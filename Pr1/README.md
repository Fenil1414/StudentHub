# StudentHub

## Project Overview

StudentHub is a web-based student portal designed to provide students with academic and campus-related information in one place.

The portal provides easy access to student profiles, subjects, timetable, attendance, assignments, notices, study materials, events, and faculty information.

## Problem Statement

Students often need to access academic information from different sources. StudentHub aims to provide a centralized platform where students can easily access important academic and campus-related information.

## Objectives

* Provide a centralized student portal.
* Provide simple and user-friendly navigation.
* Display academic information in an organized manner.
* Provide access to assignments and deadlines.
* Provide timetable and attendance information.
* Display notices and upcoming events.
* Organize study materials.

## User Roles

### Student

* View profile
* View subjects
* View timetable
* Check attendance
* View assignments
* Read notices
* Access study materials
* View events and faculty information

### Faculty

* Manage subjects
* Manage assignments
* Upload study materials
* Publish notices

### Admin

* Manage students and faculty
* Manage notices and events
* Manage subjects and portal content
